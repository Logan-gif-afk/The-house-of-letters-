V. For Shahad

Dear Shahad

Shahad,

This house exists because some feelings refuse to disappear when the page ends.

So I gave them rooms.

I gave my memories windows. I gave my poems walls. I gave my letters somewhere to wait.

And I gave the whole house the name that kept appearing in everything I wrote.

شهود.
